Horizon Atmospheric Shaders v2.0.0
Minecraft Java Edition 26.2
Iris + Sodium compatible

Features:
- Directional sunlight and moonlight
- Multi-colored block lighting (torches, lanterns, lava, glowstone, etc.)
- Per-source RGB color and intensity in Shader Pack Settings
- Color grading, bloom, and tonemapping

Installation:
1. Install Fabric/NeoForge with Sodium and Iris for MC 26.2
2. Copy this zip to .minecraft/shaderpacks/
3. Select the pack in Video Settings > Shader Packs
4. Tune block light colors under Shader Pack Settings > Block Light Colors
